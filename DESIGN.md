# Design notes

## Story

The sample film is a fictional Amharic family comedy about Uncle Tadesse's first
computer. Eight storyboarded shots establish the premise, escalate a sequence of
computer mishaps, introduce his niece, and land on a warm punchline. English
subtitles are included per shot.

## Visual and audio direction

The storyboard specifies a warm, stylized Addis Ababa living room, a recurring
character reference, seed values, motion prompts, and a playful instrumental
score. Both 16:9 and 9:16 are requested. These are creative inputs only: the
current mock implementation does not render these directions into media.

## Delivery intent

The final edit should include Amharic narration, English subtitles, original
instrumental music ducked under narration, and a visible AI disclosure. Final
encode settings and actual audio mix remain to be implemented and verified.
